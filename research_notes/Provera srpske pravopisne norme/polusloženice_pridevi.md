## 1. „crno-beli“ vs „crnobeli“: coordinate vs subordinate compound adjectives

### Takeaway
Every source found agrees on the general rule. A hyphen is used when the parts are coordinate, meaning a combination of two colours ("partly black, partly white"). The compound is written together when the first part modifies the second (a shade, e.g. *tamnozelen*, *plavozelen*). So *crno-beli film / crno-bela fotografija* takes a hyphen. Confidence: **high** for the rule, **medium** for the citation. I found no primary-text tačka number, and could not check whether *crno-beli* is listed in the PMS rečnik.

### Cited Findings
- A secondary summary of Klajn's word-formation work says colour compounds are coordinate when they name a combination (e.g. *crveno-plavi dres*) and attributive when they name a shade (*crnosmeđ*, *plavosiv*). It also says the 1993 Pravopis kept the hyphen for colour combinations. — [WebSearch summary pointing to Klajn, *Tvorba reči*, Scribd copy (text not accessible)](https://www.scribd.com/doc/230722685/Tvorba-re%C4%8Di-Ivana-Klajna-1) (secondary; I could not see the page reference)
- A Serbian school guide gives *crveno-beli dresovi* as a colour combination with a hyphen, and *mrkožut, tamnozelen, svetlocrven* written together. — [srednjeskole.edukacija.rs](https://srednjeskole.edukacija.rs/srpski-jezik/pisano-izrazavanje/spojeno-i-odvojeno-pisanje-reci) (secondary)
- A translator and lektor blog contrasts *plavo-zelen* („dotični predmet dvobojan, plav i zelen“) with *plavozelen* (a single shade), and *književno-istorijski* with *književnoistorijski*. — [marialestaric.blogspot.com, March 2013](http://marialestaric.blogspot.com/2013/03/) (secondary; recommends Pravopis 2010 and Klajn RJN but gives no tačka)
- Pismenica.rs: „Када је реч о комбинацији боја, пише се цртица“. — [pismenica.rs](https://www.pismenica.rs/boje-nijanse-kombinacije-boja/) (secondary)
- Croatian and Bosnian sources draw the same distinction (*crno-bijel* vs *crnobijel*). They are not the Serbian norm and are listed only as corroboration. — [geek.hr](https://geek.hr/pravopis/kako-se-pise/crno-bijel-crnobijel-ili-crno-bijel/); [lekturica](https://lekturica.wordpress.com/2019/10/21/pisanje-boja/)

### Inferences
- *crnobeli* is not wrong as a spelling in itself. But it would mean a single shade ("blackish-white"), which is not what *crno-beli film* means. In a benchmark item about "film, fotografija", the gold answer should be *crno-beli*.

### Gaps
- The exact PMS 2010/2017 tačka for složeni pridevi with a hyphen. **[unverified, from model memory]:** it sits in the chapter on writing compounds together and with a hyphen, near the polusloženice tačka 86. I have not confirmed the number.
- Whether *crno-beli* is a headword in the PMS 2010 pravopisni rečnik: not checked.

## 2. „naučnofantastični“ vs „naučno-fantastični“

### Takeaway
It is written together: **naučnofantastični**. The adjective comes from the phrase *naučna fantastika*, in which the first word modifies the second (a subordinate relation). Several Serbian lektor and usage sources say so explicitly and call *naučno-fantastični* non-standard. Confidence: **high** for the rule (consistent across sources, follows the general rule), **low to medium** for a primary citation.

### Cited Findings
- „Spojeno se pišu pridevske složenice od dvočlanih sintagmi u kojima prva određuje drugu“. Examples: *naučnofantastični*, *krivičnopravni*. A hyphen is used when the adjectives „ne određuju jedan drugog“ (*naučno-istraživački*, *političko-ekonomski*). — [kakosepise.com, V. Tešić, 2013](https://kakosepise.com/rec/naucnoistrazivacki-ili-naucno-istrazivacki/) (secondary; no primary citation)
- „ako želimo da napravimo pridev od sklopa naučna fantastika, on će glasiti samo naučnofantastični“. — [marialestaric.blogspot.com, March 2013](http://marialestaric.blogspot.com/2013/03/) (secondary)
- The same kakosepise.com entry says both *naučnoistraživački* and *naučno-istraživački* are acceptable, depending on meaning („Piše se i jedno i drugo“). — [kakosepise.com](https://kakosepise.com/rec/naucnoistrazivacki-ili-naucno-istrazivacki/)

### Inferences
- *naučno-fantastični* would only make sense as "scientific and fantastic" (coordinate), which no one means. For a test item, *naučnofantastični* is the safe gold answer.

### Gaps
- No primary-text tačka and no confirmed PMS rečnik headword. **[unverified, from model memory]:** *naučnofantastičan* is listed in the PMS rečnik written together. Not confirmed.

## 3. „ekspres-lonac“ / „ekspres lonac“ / „ekspreslonac“, „ekspres-pošiljka“

### Takeaway
This item is **disputed or uncertain**. Under the PMS 2010 general rule, words whose first part is invariable are polusloženice and take a hyphen (*auto-put, foto-aparat, radio-aparat*, cited as PMS t. 86a). That supports *ekspres-lonac* and *ekspres-pošiljka*. However, kakosepise.com treats *ekspres* as an invariable adjective written separately (*ekspres lonac, ekspres restoran*), and cites no source. *ekspreslonac* (written together) has no support anywhere. Confidence: **low to medium**. Without the PMS rečnik entry this cannot be settled.

### Cited Findings
- Milja Lukić (lektor), on PMS 2010: „Reči kojima je prvi deo nepromenljiv tretiraju se kao polusloženice“. Hence *auto-put* and *foto-aparat*, a change from the earlier pravopis. Polusloženice are explained in detail in **PMS t. 86 a**. — [SlovoPres / Milja Lukić, 2012](https://slovopres.com/muke-s-pravopisom-moze-i-ovako-i-onako-i-nikako/) (secondary, but cites a tačka)
- The same author says PMS writes *internet adresa, internet stranica, rok koncert, bejzbol palica* separately. So the PMS itself has cases where an invariable noun used like an adjective is written apart. She also notes internal inconsistencies, such as *fotosinteza / foto-sinteza*. — [SlovoPres](https://slovopres.com/muke-s-pravopisom-moze-i-ovako-i-onako-i-nikako/)
- „Piše se ekspres restoran“, with *ekspres* treated as an invariable adjective (example *ekspres lonac*). No source is cited. — [kakosepise.com](https://kakosepise.com/rec/ekspres-restoran-ili-ekspres-restoran/) (secondary, no citation)
- The Serbian Wikipedia style guide paraphrases the Pravopis rule: a hyphen when each part keeps its own accent and the first part does not decline. — [sr.wikipedia, Википедија:Правопис](https://sr.wikipedia.org/sr-ec/%D0%92%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%BF%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%98%D0%B0:%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%B8%D1%81) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Two readings conflict. In one, *ekspres* is a noun-like first part (polusloženica, hyphen). In the other, it is an invariable adjective (written apart, like *internet adresa*). The PMS's own treatment of *internet adresa* shows that the "invariable adjective, written separately" route exists in the norm. **[unverified, from model memory]:** PMS 2010 allows both the hyphenated and the separate spelling for some such first parts. Not confirmed.
- Recommendation for a benchmark: do not use *ekspres-lonac* vs *ekspres lonac* as the deciding contrast in an exact-match item. Only *ekspreslonac* is safely wrong.

### Gaps
- The PMS 2010/2017 rečnik entries for *ekspres* (and *ekspres-lonac*, *ekspres-pošiljka*): not found.
- What Klajn's RJN says under *ekspres*: not found.

## 4. „vikend-kuća“ vs „vikend kuća“

### Takeaway
By the PMS 2010 polusloženice rule (invariable first part, hyphen; t. 86a per a secondary source), *vikend-kuća* is the expected normative form. I found no source, primary or secondary, that addresses this word. Confidence: **medium** for the rule-based inference, **low** for a direct citation.

### Cited Findings
- General rule: invariable first part means a polusloženica written with a hyphen (PMS t. 86a). — [SlovoPres / Milja Lukić](https://slovopres.com/muke-s-pravopisom-moze-i-ovako-i-onako-i-nikako/) (secondary)
- The linguistics glossary defines a polusloženica as two words joined with a hyphen (*rak-rana, radio-aparat, remek-delo, gas-maska*). — [lingvistickitermini.rs](https://www.lingvistickitermini.rs/pojmovnik/poluslozenica/) (secondary/reference)
- Usage in the wild is split. Real-estate listings write „VIKEND KUĆA“ separately. — [svilajnacnekretnine.com](https://svilajnacnekretnine.com/nekretnines/vikend-kuca-tip-44b/) (usage only, not normative)

### Inferences
- The same tension as with *ekspres* applies (compare *internet adresa*, *rok koncert* written separately in PMS). *vikend-kuća* has strong rule support. Whether *vikend kuća* is also allowed is unconfirmed. Note: *vikendica* is the one-word alternative.

### Gaps
- Not checked whether *vikend-kuća* is a headword in the PMS rečnik. **[unverified, from model memory]:** it is listed with a hyphen. Not confirmed.

## General rules (summary)
- **Polusloženice:** a hyphen when the first part is invariable and each part keeps its own accent. PMS 2010 **t. 86 (a)**, as cited by [SlovoPres](https://slovopres.com/muke-s-pravopisom-moze-i-ovako-i-onako-i-nikako/), not checked in the primary text. **Note:** the brief's "t. 52–60" range is **not** confirmed. The only number found is t. 86a.
- **Složeni pridevi:** coordinate relation (X and Y) takes a hyphen (*crno-beli, naučno-istraživački, srpsko-engleski*). Subordinate relation (X modifies Y, from a phrase) is written together (*naučnofantastični, krivičnopravni, tamnozelen*). Sources: [kakosepise.com](https://kakosepise.com/rec/naucnoistrazivacki-ili-naucno-istrazivacki/), [marialestaric](http://marialestaric.blogspot.com/2013/03/), [srednjeskole.edukacija.rs](https://srednjeskole.edukacija.rs/srpski-jezik/pisano-izrazavanje/spojeno-i-odvojeno-pisanje-reci). All secondary.
- **Further research:** [Dražić & Ajdžanović (2010), "Problemi u pisanju složenica i polusloženica u srpskom i hrvatskom pravopisu"](https://enauka.gov.rs/handle/123456789/242122?locale=en) (only the metadata was available) is a possible academic source for further checking.
