## Q1. foto-aparat / auto-put / radio-stanica: Pravopis 2010 vs Klajn vs Pravopis 1993

### Takeaway
Pravopis 2010 hyphenates all three: **фото-апарат, ауто-пут, радио-станица** (t. 84, pp. 78–79, high confidence). Klajn's RJN, which follows the 1993 Pravopis ("P"), gives **fotoaparat** (foto- "always joined") and prefers **autoput**. So Klajn/1993 and 2010 disagree on foto-aparat and auto-put (medium confidence, because I only saw a garbled excerpt of the RJN). On radio-stanica everyone uses the hyphen. Klajn reviewed the 2010 Pravopis, so this looks like a change between 1993 and 2010, not an open dispute.

### Cited Findings
- **The general rule, P2010 t. 82 (pp. 75–76):** a hyphen marks combinations whose parts keep "известан степен значењске аутономности" and whose first part does not change. Examples: ауто-кућа, радио-техничар — [Pravopis 2010, t. 82, p. 76](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf)
- **P2010 t. 83 (p. 77):** it treats ауто, авио, аеро, мото, фото, радио, видео, стерео, аудио, кино, макро, микро, макси, мини, екстра, супер, квази… as a special normative problem ("формант" or "префиксоид"). It admits the norm often fell back on "појединачном, лексичком нормирању". The main criterion for hyphenation is formal: the first part does not change. Same source, t. 83, p. 77.
- **ауто-1 ("сам, сопствени") is written solid** (аутобиографија, аутопортрет) — P2010 t. 83, p. 77; dictionary headword *ауто-1*, p. 261
- **ауто-2 (= аутомобил, аутомобилски): t. 84 (p. 78) lists only аутодром and аутострада as solid.** The hyphenated list starts with **ауто-пут**, ауто-цеста, ауто-механичар, ауто-стоп… The dictionary headword *ауто-2 (б)* (pp. 261–262) also lists ауто-пут and ауто-стоп. No joined "аутопут" appears anywhere in the text. — [Pravopis 2010, t. 84, p. 78; rečnik pp. 261–262](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf)
- **фото1 ("светлосни") is solid** (фотосинтеза, фототерапија, фотоћелија). **фото2 ("фотографски") is solid only "када је друга саставница несамостална"** (фотограф, фотографија, фототека). Otherwise it takes the hyphen: **фото-апарат**, фото-модел, фото-копија, фото-репортер, фото-монтажа… — P2010 t. 84, p. 79
- **Internal inconsistency in P2010:** the dictionary headword *фото-* (p. 485) puts фото-синтеза, фото-терапија, фото-ћелија under the hyphenated list. That contradicts t. 84. Фото-апарат itself is not listed under the headword. — [Pravopis 2010 rečnik p. 485](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf). Brborić flags the same mismatch: "постоји неслагање између примера датих у речнику и основном тексту" — [Brborić 2016, MSC 45/3, p. 12–13 (secondary)](https://fil.bg.ac.rs/uploads/files/Strane/Fakultet/Centri-i-instituti/MSC/Nau%C4%8Dni-sastanak-slavista-u-Vukove-dane/MSC%2045-3.pdf)
- **радио1 (radiation, or a dependent second part) is solid:** радиоактивност, радиологија. **радио2 ("радијски") takes the hyphen: радио-станица**, радио-апарат, радио-пријемник, радио-техничар… When радио means "радијска установа" it is written as a separate word: Радио Београд. Радио-телевизија Београд has a hyphen. — P2010 t. 84, p. 79; dictionary headword *радио-*, p. 432
- **видео and мини, P2010 t. 84 (pp. 79–80):** видео-касета, видео-игра, видео-рикордер (и само видео). мини is solid only in минибус; otherwise мини-сукња, мини-серија, мини-голф… — Pravopis 2010 t. 84, pp. 79–80; dictionary headword *мини-* (rečnik)
- **Klajn RJN, *auto-* (excerpt):** solid in the "sopstven" sense; "S crticom se piše u značenju 'automobilski'" (auto-trke, auto-servis). But for words felt as one unit (autoput, automehaničar, autostop) "P daje prednost spojenom pisanju" — [Klajn, RJN, OCR copy via search excerpt](https://pdfcoffee.com/ivan-klajn-renik-jezikih-nedoumica-9-pdf-free.html) (403 when fetched directly; text seen only in a search-engine summary)
- **Klajn RJN, *foto-* (excerpt):** "po P uvek spojeno, bez obzira na značenje" (fotoaparat, fotoreporter, fotomontaža, fotokopija). The hyphen is preferred only in "slobodnim i slučajnim vezama" (foto-atelje, foto-izložba, foto-materijal) — [same OCR copy](https://pdfcoffee.com/ivan-klajn-renik-jezikih-nedoumica-9-pdf-free.html); copies are also on [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/388861212/340914921-%D0%98%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD-%D0%9A%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%98%D0%BD-Ivan-Klajn-%D0%A0%D0%B5%D1%87%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BA-%D0%88%D0%B5%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%87%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%85-%D0%9D%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%83%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B0-Re%C4%8Dnik-Jezi%C4%8Dkih-Nedoumica-Cir-PDF) and [Issuu](https://issuu.com/ljubicaradovanovicnikolic/docs/45733154-recnik-jezickih-nedoumica/52) (not opened)
- **Klajn RJN, *radio-*:** I found no excerpt of the entry. Popular secondary guides give radio-stanica with a hyphen for radio meaning the device — [resilako.com (secondary, popular)](https://resilako.com/obrazovanje/gramatika/kako-se-pravilno-pise/)
- **Ivan Klajn is named as reviewer ("Рецензенти: Иван Клајн, Драго Ћупић") of Pravopis 2010** — [Pravopis 2010, p. 4](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf)
- **Change in structure:** P1993 had 236 tačke and P2010 has 291 "правописна правила", so tačka numbers do not carry over between editions — [Brborić 2016, p. 7 (secondary)](https://fil.bg.ac.rs/uploads/files/Strane/Fakultet/Centri-i-instituti/MSC/Nau%C4%8Dni-sastanak-slavista-u-Vukove-dane/MSC%2045-3.pdf)
- **2017:** Serbian Wikipedia lists 2011, 2013 and 2017 as the 5th, 6th and 7th editions of the same "измењено и допуњено издање" — [sr.wikipedia, Правопис српскога језика](https://sr.wikipedia.org/sr-ec/%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%B8%D1%81_%D1%81%D1%80%D0%BF%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%B0_%D1%98%D0%B5%D0%B7%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B0). Brborić quotes the 2013 printing's note that it only fixed "ретке словне и техничке грешке" (Pravopis 2013: 8) — [Brborić 2016, p. 6–7](https://fil.bg.ac.rs/uploads/files/Strane/Fakultet/Centri-i-instituti/MSC/Nau%C4%8Dni-sastanak-slavista-u-Vukove-dane/MSC%2045-3.pdf)

### Inferences
- **For a benchmark gold under the current official norm** (P2010, which the Odbor za standardizaciju sponsored per p. 4): **фото-апарат, ауто-пут, радио-станица (also видео-касета, мини-сукња)** are correct. Fotoaparat and autoput are the 1993/Klajn-RJN forms. High confidence for P2010. Medium confidence that the 1993 Pravopis prescribed fotoaparat and autoput, because that rests only on Klajn's "po P" in an RJN excerpt. That RJN predates 2010 and "P" most likely means P1993, but I did not confirm the edition.
- **Klajn reviewed P2010, so he presumably accepted the hyphenated forms in 2010.** Treat the disagreement as RJN (pre-2010) vs P2010, not as Klajn's current position. Whether later RJN editions were brought into line with P2010 is unverified.
- **Fotosinteza/fotoćelija are ambiguous inside P2010 itself:** solid in t. 84, hyphenated in the rečnik. Items that hinge on them should be avoided or accept both.
- **Radio-stanica is the same across all sources I found**, so it is a safe item.

### Gaps
- I could not open the 1993/1994 Pravopis. Its ruling on fotoaparat/autoput rests only on Klajn's "po P" excerpt, and I have no tačka or page.
- I have no exact edition or page for the RJN entries, and the excerpt was garbled OCR passed through a search summarizer. The RJN *radio-* entry was not found.
- Klajn, *Tvorba reči u savremenom srpskom jeziku*: I found no accessible text on foto-/auto-/radio-.
- 2017 printing: I found no source listing changes to t. 83–84. The edition history suggests a reprint with corrections, but this is unverified.
- I found no Odbor za standardizaciju decision on these words.

## Q2. Ijekavian greška vs grješka (short jat after consonant + r)

### Takeaway
Pravopis 2010 **t. 8д (p. 24)** allows only **грешка** in ijekavian. Grješka is not an allowed doublet. Short jat after a consonant group ending in р gives е in both pronunciations (брегови, времена, стрелица, грешка). The only ijekavian doublets allowed are prefix-boundary cases like разрјешење/разрешење. High confidence. The long syllable stays ије: бријег, стријела.

### Cited Findings
- **P2010 t. 8д (p. 24):** "у кратким слоговима иза групе сугласника чији је задњи члан р", the ekavian/ijekavian difference is neutralized and jat is replaced by е. Examples: бреза, брегови, времена, вреднији, **грешка, грешан, грешник**, мрежа, огрев, стрелица, срећа, стреха, требати, трешња, црепови — [Pravopis 2010, t. 8д, p. 24](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf)
- **Note (1) to t. 8д:** when р has no consonant before it, the Vukovian forms are the only correct ones: горјети, рјечник, рјешавати, корјенчић, старјешина. The note says the ekavian forms common in ijekavian speech are not standard (except parallel корјенчић/коренчић and старјешина/старешина) — same source, p. 24
- **Note (2) to t. 8д:** doublets are allowed only where the boundary between parts is still felt: разређивати/разрјеђивати, разрешење/разрјешење — same source, p. 24
- **The dictionary agrees:** headword *грешка* "(ек. и ијек.), т. 8д" (p. 292); *грех… ек. и ијек.: грешан, грешка, грешник… в. т. 8д* (p. 292). *бријег, мн. брегови* (p. 274; "песн." бријези). *стријела, стријелац (ијек.)* vs ek. стрела (p. 462). No *грјешка* or *брјегови* entry exists — [Pravopis 2010 rečnik](https://jelenaradomir.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/pravopis-ms_2010.pdf)
- **Secondary, history:** Vuk's 1852 Rječnik had грјешка, грјехота, грјешник, стрјелица. Normative handbooks (Pravopis 1960, Pravopis 1993, RSJ 2007) later made them uniform as грешан, грешка, погрешка: "уједначен је правописним приручницима (ПС, ПН, ЈР)". In speech the е forms are stable, and грје- forms are sporadic — [Lončar Raičević 2013, Niš, pp. 305, 310](https://izdanja.filfak.ni.ac.rs/casopisi/2013/download/1079_eb4a7bd0770e8beaf2aa9ef7d388c8c1)
- **Secondary:** Vuković's *Pravopisna pravila* (1949) also gives грешан, грехота, грешка — [same paper, p. 305](https://izdanja.filfak.ni.ac.rs/casopisi/2013/download/1079_eb4a7bd0770e8beaf2aa9ef7d388c8c1)

### Inferences
- **For ijekavian items in the Serbian standard, грешка is the only correct form and грјешка is an error.** P1993 (per the secondary source) and P2010 agree, and I found no source where Klajn disagrees. "Strijela/strela" is not a jat-after-consonant+r case in the short syllable. Long jat gives стријела (ijek.), and the short-syllable derivative is стрелица.
- **Брјегови is non-standard; the form is брегови.** Grješka belongs to the Vukovian/older or regional (and Montenegrin-norm) tradition, not to the Serbian standard.

### Gaps
- I did not see the tačka number for this rule in P1993, only the secondary statement that P1993 ("ПН") made it uniform.
- I found no explicit Klajn RJN entry on greška/grješka.
- I could not check the 2017 printing directly. I found no evidence that it changed t. 8д.
